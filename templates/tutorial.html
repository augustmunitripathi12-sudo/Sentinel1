🛡️ How Sentinel Works

1

Create an Account

Sign up with your username, email, and password. You'll get a verification email to confirm you're real.

2

Secure Login with 2FA

Every time you log in, we send a 4-digit code to your email. Enter it to access your chats.

3

Find Your Finder ID

Go to Settings (⚙) to see your unique Finder ID. Share it with friends so they can add you.

4

Add Friends

Tap the + button and enter your friend's Finder ID. Once added, you can start messaging instantly.

5

Send Messages & Voice Notes

Type text messages or tap the 🎤 mic button to record and send voice messages — just like WhatsApp.
